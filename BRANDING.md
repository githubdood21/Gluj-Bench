## Independently distributed forks

- Use a distinct product name and logo for an independently distributed fork or modified build. Do not market it as an official Gluj-Bench release or use the Gluj-Bench name or logo as your product's branding without written permission.
- Clearly identify your distributor or maintainer and state that the version is modified and unofficial. Preserve the modification notices and dates required by GPLv3.
- Preserve required copyright, license and attribution notices. Rebranding does not allow removing those notices or changing the license of covered code.
- You may factually describe your product as "based on Gluj-Bench" or "a fork of Gluj-Bench", with a link to the original project, provided that your own product identity is prominent and you do not imply affiliation or endorsement.

You may redistribute unmodified copies under GPLv3 and retain their existing embedded name, logo and origin notices. Identify yourself as the redistributor, and do not imply that your distribution channel, sale, support service or warranty is operated or endorsed by the original project.

Accurate references to the project in reviews, articles, benchmark results, compatibility statements and attribution are permitted. This policy does not limit rights that applicable law independently permits, including lawful descriptive or nominative use.

## Commercial distribution

GPLv3 permits commercial use and selling copies, including unchanged copies. This policy does not require substantial changes before a copy may be sold and does not impose a commercial-license fee.

The project discourages charging for unchanged or minimally modified copies of software that is freely available from the original project. We encourage commercial derivatives to offer substantial improvements to core functionality and distinct branding. This paragraph expresses a preference, not an additional license condition.
